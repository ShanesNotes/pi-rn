# ICU consult note — 06:25

## Reason for consult
- Septic shock with worsening respiratory failure despite fluid resuscitation and HFNC

## Brief assessment
68F admitted from ED with severe community-acquired pneumonia and septic shock. Despite ~1.3 L of a planned 30 mL/kg crystalloid bolus and HFNC at 40 L / 50% FiO2, MAP remains 64 with persistent tachycardia and lactate elevation [evt-002-0037, evt-002-0038]. Respiratory trajectory is worsening with accessory muscle use [evt-002-0032] and ROX index borderline.

## Recommendations
- Accept for MICU admission, bed 8.
- On arrival: place RIJ central line and left radial arterial line at bedside.
- Initiate norepinephrine titrated to MAP ≥ 65.
- Foley for strict hourly I&O.
- Continue current antibiotics; pharmacy renal-dose review.
- Anticipate possible intubation if respiratory trajectory continues; have bedside set-up ready.

## Disposition
- Accepted to MICU; transfer pending bed availability.

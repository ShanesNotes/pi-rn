# Discharge summary — 2022-08-08

## Admission and discharge dates
2022-08-03 to 2022-08-08 (5 days).

## Discharge diagnoses
1. Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, new diagnosis.
2. Hypertensive urgency on presentation.
3. Acute kidney injury on chronic kidney disease, resolved.

## Hospital course (brief)
A 64-year-old woman with longstanding hypertension, T2DM, and CKD
3a presented with two weeks of progressive lower extremity edema
and exertional dyspnea. She was found to be hypertensive to 188/102,
with elevated BNP and chest x-ray showing pulmonary congestion and
small bilateral effusions. Echocardiogram demonstrated EF 58% with
grade 2 diastolic dysfunction, consistent with HFpEF. She was
treated with IV furosemide diuresis with steady improvement; her
admission Cr of 1.6 returned to her likely baseline of 1.3 by
discharge. Cardiology was consulted, established HFpEF diagnosis,
recommended ongoing diuresis and outpatient follow-up. The patient
lost approximately 5 kg of fluid weight over 5 days. Her home BP
regimen was adjusted: lisinopril continued, amlodipine continued.
Metoprolol succinate 25 mg daily added for rate control and BP
support; furosemide 20 mg daily added as outpatient diuretic. She
was educated on daily standing weights, sodium restriction, and
HF action plan.

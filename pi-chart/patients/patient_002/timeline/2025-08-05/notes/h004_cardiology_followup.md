# Cardiology follow-up — 2025-08-05

## Patient
67-year-old woman with HFpEF (dx 2022 [enc-002-h001]), HTN, T2DM, and
CKD 3a, here for annual cardiology follow-up.

## Subjective
She is doing well from a cardiac standpoint and remains essentially
asymptomatic. NYHA class II at baseline — she walks daily, does
weekly water aerobics without difficulty, and can walk to her mailbox
and back without dyspnea. She has not had any orthopnea, paroxysmal
nocturnal dyspnea, peripheral edema, palpitations, presyncope, or
chest pain since her 2022 hospitalization. She has been weighing
daily on the same scale (a habit from her HF education) and the
weight has been steady at 65 ± 1 kg. Home BPs run 130s/70s by her
log. She tolerates her medications well.

She brought up the SGLT2 inhibitor question; she has been on the
waiting list with her insurance for prior authorization for
empagliflozin. We discussed the rationale (mortality and renal
benefit in HFpEF, T2DM, and CKD; modest BP and weight reduction;
relevant in her case) and she remains amenable. I will write a fresh
note to PCP for coordination.

## Past medical history
HFpEF (EF 58%), HTN, T2DM, CKD 3a, GERD, mild background diabetic
retinopathy.

## Medications
Metformin, amlodipine, losartan, furosemide, omeprazole,
atorvastatin, ASA 81, vitamin D, multivitamin. No recent changes. No
beta-blocker (metoprolol was discontinued at the post-discharge
visit in 2022 for fatigue; she has not been on any BB since).
Spironolactone trial in 2023 stopped for hyperkalemia at K 5.4.

## Allergies
NKDA.

## Objective
- Vitals: BP 138/78, HR 68, weight 65.0 kg, BMI 25.7
  [evt-002-h004-001].
- General: well-appearing.
- Cardiovascular: regular, no murmurs, S1/S2 normal, no S3/S4 today.
  Carotids without bruits. JVP not elevated.
- Pulmonary: clear bilaterally.
- Extremities: no edema. Pulses 2+. No cyanosis.
- Neurologic: A&O × 3.

## Point-of-care echo
A focused 2D transthoracic echocardiogram was performed today. EF
58% (Simpson biplane). Grade 1 diastolic dysfunction (improved from
grade 2 at presentation in 2022). Mild left atrial enlargement (LAVI
36, slightly improved from 38 in 2022). Mild concentric LVH unchanged.
Trace MR and trace TR. Estimated PASP 32 mmHg. IVC normal in size and
collapsibility [evt-002-h004-003].

## Assessment and plan
1. **HFpEF, stable, NYHA II** [evt-002-h004-004]. Echo today is
   modestly improved from her 2022 study, consistent with the
   antihypertensive control and diuresis maintained over the past
   three years. Continue losartan, amlodipine, furosemide.
2. **Chronic HTN.** Office BP today 138/78; home log avg ~130s/70s.
   I am satisfied with her current regimen plus the proposed SGLT2i
   addition. Will defer further titration to PCP.
3. **SGLT2 inhibitor (empagliflozin 10 mg daily proposed).** Pending
   insurance prior auth. I will send a fresh PA letter and coordinate
   with PCP to start once approved. She is at low risk for euglycemic
   DKA given her current glycemic control.
4. **Beta-blocker.** Not currently on. Given she is asymptomatic with
   well-controlled rate, I will not push to restart a beta-blocker
   given her past intolerance.
5. **Aldosterone antagonist.** Trialed in 2023, stopped for
   hyperkalemia. Will not retry while CKD persists.
6. **Lipids.** LDL 92 on last labs (2024-09 PCP) — at goal on
   atorvastatin 40.
7. **Volume status.** Stable. No diuretic adjustment.
8. **Health maintenance.** Up to date per PCP.

## Follow-up
Annual cardiology follow-up in 12 months. Patient knows to call sooner
for any HF symptoms (recurrent dyspnea, edema, weight gain >2 lb in
1 day or >5 lb in a week). Will message PCP regarding SGLT2i.

# Nephrology follow-up — 2024-03-15

## Patient
66-year-old woman with CKD stage 3a, hypertension, type 2 diabetes,
and HFpEF, here for annual nephrology follow-up.

## Subjective
She is doing well overall. No new symptoms. No edema. No recent
nausea, fatigue out of baseline, or new pruritus. She continues
to walk daily, attends water aerobics weekly, and has resumed all of
her usual activities without limitation. She has been adherent to her
medications including her ARB and home diuretic. Home BPs in the past
month average 138/80 per her log; she says her PCP is "tweaking" her
BP regimen at the next visit. She has had no recent illnesses, no
contrast studies, and no NSAID use.

She specifically asks about whether she needs dialysis or a transplant
referral. I reassure her that her kidney function is stable and we are
not in the territory where those conversations are required, but that
we will continue to follow her annually.

## Past medical history
T2DM, HTN, HFpEF (EF 58%), CKD 3a, GERD. Surgical history per chart.

## Medications
Reviewed and reconciled: metformin 1000 BID, amlodipine 5, losartan 50,
furosemide 20, omeprazole 20, atorvastatin 40, ASA 81, vitamin D 2000,
multivitamin. No NSAIDs. No herbal supplements. No PPI changes.

## Allergies
NKDA.

## Objective
- Vitals: BP 142/80, HR 76, weight 64.8 kg (stable from last year),
  BMI 25.6 [evt-002-h002-001].
- General: well-appearing, no apparent distress.
- HEENT: no scleral icterus, no dry mucous membranes.
- Cardiovascular: regular rate and rhythm, no murmur, JVP not
  elevated.
- Pulmonary: clear bilaterally.
- Abdomen: soft, non-tender.
- Extremities: no peripheral edema. Pulses 2+. AV access not
  applicable.
- Skin: no uremic findings.

## Pre-visit labs (collected 2024-03-12)
- BMP: Na 140, K 4.4, Cl 104, CO2 24, BUN 24, Cr 1.4 (eGFR 42)
  [evt-002-h002-002].
- Urine albumin/creatinine ratio 42 mg/g (microalbuminuria,
  similar to last year) [evt-002-h002-003].
- Phosphorus 3.6, calcium 9.2, intact PTH 68, hemoglobin 12.4,
  bicarbonate 24, 25-OH vitamin D 34 [evt-002-h002-004].

## Assessment and plan
1. **CKD stage 3a, stable.** Cr 1.4 for the third year in a row;
   eGFR 42. PTH mildly elevated but unchanged from prior. No
   metabolic acidosis. Hgb at the lower end of normal but stable.
   Microalbuminuria persistent at ~40 mg/g, consistent with combined
   diabetic-hypertensive nephropathy [evt-002-h002-005].
2. **Hypertension.** Office BP today 142/80; home log average 138/80.
   Suboptimal. Will defer titration to PCP/cardiology since she sees
   them more frequently.
3. **Renoprotection.** Continue losartan at current dose. Consider
   adding an SGLT2 inhibitor (empagliflozin or dapagliflozin) given
   her T2DM, HFpEF, and microalbuminuria — these have demonstrated
   renal and cardiovascular benefit in this profile. Will write to
   the PCP and cardiology to coordinate.
4. **Avoid NSAIDs.** Patient reports she does not use them; reinforced.
5. **Contrast.** No current contraindication to standard contrast
   imaging if needed; pre-procedure hydration recommended.
6. **Follow-up.** Return in 12 months with repeat BMP and UACR. Will
   message with results in 1 week. Patient should call sooner if any
   new symptoms (worsening edema, decreased urine output, fatigue, or
   confusion).

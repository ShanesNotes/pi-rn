# Family update — hospital day 2 morning

## Recipient
Patient's husband (HCP), at bedside.

## Summary of conversation
Husband arrived at 09:00 and was at bedside through morning rounds.
I gave him the following update from overnight and the morning:

- Patient remained intubated and sedated overnight. Sedation has
  been lightening — she is now arousable to her name and follows
  simple commands such as squeezing my hand, with mouthing through
  the tube. RASS −2 currently.
- Lactate has continued to normalize and is now 1.4 (down from 4.6
  on admission); this is a key marker of her shock improving.
- Urine output has improved to ~50 mL/hr (was much lower yesterday).
  Her kidney function is also improving — Cr 1.7, down from a peak
  of 2.1 yesterday. The kidney specialist saw her this morning and
  was very encouraged.
- Norepinephrine drip (the medicine to support her blood pressure)
  has been titrated down from 0.05 to 0.03 mcg/kg/min over the
  morning. We are hopeful this will be off entirely by evening.
- Vancomycin trough was sent this morning to ensure dose is
  appropriate for her kidneys.
- Antibiotics continue.
- ID has been consulted — they will see her later today.
- Plan for tomorrow morning is to do an awakening trial (lighten
  sedation) and a breathing trial (let her show us she can breathe
  on her own); if she passes both we will work toward removing
  the breathing tube.

## Husband's questions
- "What does the breathing trial look like?" — explained the
  spontaneous breathing trial: switch the ventilator to a low
  pressure-support setting and let her do most of the breathing
  work for 30 minutes to 2 hours; we look at her work of breathing,
  oxygenation, and tolerance.
- "Will she be confused when she wakes up?" — explained that some
  delirium is common, especially for the first day or two; we will
  watch for it and treat as needed; nighttime sleep and family at
  bedside both help.
- "Should our daughter come from out of town?" — daughter is local
  but adult son is coming from St. Louis; we suggested that based
  on the trajectory it would be reasonable for him to come this
  weekend, though things can always change.

## Code status
Reviewed; remains full code. No changes.

## Plan
Husband will stay through midday, return for evening. Will be called
for any significant change.

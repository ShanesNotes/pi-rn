# Procedure note — right internal jugular triple-lumen central venous catheter

**Indication.** Need for vasopressor administration in septic shock with peripheral access only.

**Consent.** Verbal consent obtained from patient's husband (healthcare proxy) given patient's altered mentation; risks/benefits/alternatives discussed including bleeding, infection, pneumothorax, arterial puncture, arrhythmia. Patient was alert enough to nod assent.

**Time-out.** Performed with bedside RN.

**Technique.**
- Patient supine, mild Trendelenburg.
- Right neck prepped with chlorhexidine, full sterile drape, mask, hat, sterile gown, sterile gloves; bedside team also masked.
- Ultrasound used to identify the right IJ vein lateral to the carotid; vein compressed easily and was non-thrombosed.
- Lidocaine 1% to skin and subcutaneous tissue.
- Single attempt with introducer needle under direct ultrasound visualization; venous blood return.
- Wire passed without resistance; ultrasound confirmed wire in vein in long axis. Skin nicked, dilator passed and removed, triple-lumen catheter passed over the wire to 15 cm at the skin. All three lumens drew back blood freely and flushed with saline.
- Catheter sutured at 15 cm; sterile dressing applied.

**Confirmation.** No arterial backflow. No air aspirated. Position to be confirmed on post-procedure CXR.

**Complications.** None.

# Procedure note — endotracheal intubation

**Indication.** Impending respiratory failure in septic shock, with worsening mentation, persistent hypoxemia and acidemia (pH 7.26 / pCO2 38 / pO2 62) despite HFNC at 60 L / 80% FiO2 [evt-002-0057, evt-002-0055].

**Consent.** Emergent indication. Husband (HCP) verbally consented at the bedside.

**Pre-oxygenation.** BVM with 100% O2 with two-handed mask seal x 5 minutes prior to induction. Pre-procedure SpO2 increased to 98% [vitals 09:15].

**Medications administered (RSI).** [evt-002-0059]
- Fentanyl 100 mcg IV push
- Etomidate 20 mg IV push
- Rocuronium 75 mg IV push

**Technique.**
- Direct laryngoscopy with Mac 3 blade.
- Cormack-Lehane grade 1 view.
- 7.5 mm cuffed ETT passed through the cords on first attempt by the ICU attending.
- Cuff inflated; ETT secured at 22 cm at the lip.

**Confirmation.**
- Square-wave EtCO2 capnography on the first breath.
- Bilateral breath sounds equal.
- Misting in the tube.
- Stable vital signs through the procedure (lowest SpO2 94%).

**Post-procedure.**
- Initial vent settings AC/VC, TV 380 mL (6 mL/kg PBW 63 kg), set RR 22, PEEP 8, FiO2 1.0 [evt-002-0061].
- Sedation initiated with propofol and fentanyl infusions targeting RASS −2 to −3 [evt-002-0063, evt-002-0064].
- Post-intubation ABG and CXR ordered.

**Complications.** None.

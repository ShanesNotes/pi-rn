# Nutrition consult — hospital day 2

## Reason for consult
Tube feed initiation and nutrition target for an intubated 68-year-old
woman with septic shock secondary to severe CAP, T2DM, HFpEF, and
AKI on CKD 3a.

## Subjective
Reviewed chart and discussed with the bedside RN. Patient was
intubated yesterday morning and has been NPO since. OG tube placed
at intubation; gastric residuals so far minimal.

Per chart, baseline weight 65 kg, height 159 cm, BMI 25.7. Stable
weight at home over the past 5 years per PCP note from 2025-09-15
[evt-002-h005-001]. No history of malnutrition, eating disorder, or
recent significant unintentional weight loss. No swallowing problems
at baseline.

Diet at home is described as "Mediterranean-ish, low salt" per
patient and husband, in keeping with HF dietary guidance.

## Estimated needs
- **Energy**: ~25 kcal/kg/day actual body weight = ~1500 kcal/day
  (consistent with critical illness predictive equations; will
  reassess as patient mobilizes).
- **Protein**: 1.2–1.5 g/kg/day = ~80–100 g/day given critical illness
  and the catabolic state of sepsis. AKI on CKD does not warrant
  protein restriction in this acute setting; she has reasonable urine
  output and no plans for RRT.
- **Fluid**: managed by ICU team; we will follow their lead given
  HFpEF and recent fluid resuscitation.
- **Sodium**: limit added sodium to ≤2 g/day; consistent with home
  practice.
- **Phosphorus and potassium**: choose a renal-friendly polymeric
  formula given CKD; monitor labs.

## Recommendations
1. Initiate continuous tube feeds via OG with a renal-friendly
   polymeric formula at 1.0 kcal/mL.
2. Start at trophic rate of 20 mL/hr; advance by 20 mL/hr every 4
   hours as tolerated to a goal rate of 50 mL/hr (about 1200
   kcal/day; will fall short of full target initially, plan a higher
   protein content adjustment within 48 h).
3. Hold tube feeds for gastric residual volume >300 mL or signs of
   ileus; otherwise continue.
4. Head of bed elevated to 30 degrees while tube feeds running;
   chlorhexidine oral care q4h for VAP prevention.
5. Monitor electrolytes daily; refeeding risk is low given her
   normal BMI and recent intake but worth watching for low
   phosphorus and magnesium in the first 48 hours.
6. Reassess at 48–72 hours; will increase protein supplementation
   if she remains intubated and approaching extubation timeline.
7. When extubated and able to tolerate PO, transition to a 2 g
   sodium cardiac diet as appropriate.

## Plan
Will follow up at 48 hours.

# Family update — hospital day 3 evening

## Recipients
Patient's husband (HCP) and adult son (visiting from out of town).

## Setting
Bedside, ICU room 8. Patient awake and participating.

## Summary
Met with husband and son to review the day's events and plan.

- **Extubation**: she was extubated this morning at 08:30 after
  passing both an awakening trial and a spontaneous breathing trial.
  She has been on high-flow nasal cannula since, weaned from 30
  liters to 20 liters / 30% FiO2 by mid-afternoon.
- **Pressors**: norepinephrine was off as of yesterday afternoon and
  blood pressure has been stable since.
- **Kidney function**: returned to her baseline of 1.4.
- **Antibiotics**: blood cultures grew a single bottle that the
  microbiology team felt was likely a contaminant; the infectious
  disease team agreed and we have narrowed her antibiotics to
  ceftriaxone, the standard treatment for community-acquired
  pneumonia. Plan is to complete a course through Friday 4/24.
- **Diet**: speech therapy cleared her for a regular diet and thin
  liquids; she had a soft snack this afternoon and tolerated it
  well.
- **Activity**: PT/OT saw her today and is starting bedside mobility
  work.
- **Lines**: arterial line out today; central venous line and OG
  tube also out. Foley remains in for now and will come out on the
  floor.

## Plan
- Tonight: close monitoring; we expect a quiet night.
- Tomorrow morning: if she does well overnight (no increased work of
  breathing, no recurrent fever, oxygen need not climbing), plan is
  for transfer to the medical floor.
- Anticipated 2–3 days on the floor before discharge home with home
  health and home physical therapy.
- Outpatient follow-ups will be arranged with PCP, cardiology, and
  nephrology.

## Family questions
- Son asked about long-term lung consequences. I explained that the
  expectation is full recovery for most patients with severe
  community-acquired pneumonia, with some residual cough or fatigue
  that resolves over weeks.
- Husband asked about home medications. I explained that some of her
  chronic medications are currently held (metformin, losartan,
  amlodipine, furosemide) and that we will reassess each at discharge
  with detailed instructions; we will likely resume some and have
  her PCP make final adjustments at the post-discharge visit.

## Code status
Reviewed and remains full code; no changes.

## Disposition
Family understands and supports the plan. Husband will stay through
the evening; son will return tomorrow morning.

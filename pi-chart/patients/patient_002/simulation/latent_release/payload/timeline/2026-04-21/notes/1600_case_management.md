# Case management note — discharge planning

Initial discharge planning evaluation prior to anticipated transfer
to medical floor.

Lives with husband (also 68, retired engineer, in good health) in
a single-story home with one entry step. Two adult children — son
in St. Louis (driving in this weekend), daughter local. Husband is
the named healthcare power of attorney.

## Met with
Patient and husband at bedside, ~16:00.

## Functional and home situation
- Single-story home, no internal stairs.
- One step at front entry.
- Bedroom and bathroom on main floor.
- Husband able to assist with ADLs; he prepares most meals at home.
- She manages her own medications with husband's support setting up
  a weekly pill box.
- Drives locally; will need to defer driving until cleared by PCP.
- Functional status at baseline: independent in ADLs, ambulates with
  cane outdoors. PT today supports return-to-baseline trajectory
  [evt-002-0194].

## Insurance
Medicare Part A and B with supplemental plan; verified active.
Pharmacy benefit through Medicare Part D plan; verified active.
Medication coverage at home pharmacy reviewed.

## Anticipated disposition
Home with husband, no skilled nursing facility need.

## Services anticipated at discharge
- Home health for medication review and reinforcement (recent
  medication changes including hold/resume decisions for some chronic
  meds).
- Home PT for one to two weeks for reconditioning, then transition
  to independent home exercise.
- Outpatient follow-up arrangements:
  - PCP within 1 week.
  - Cardiology in 4–6 weeks (Dr. Okafor).
  - Nephrology in 4–6 weeks (Dr. Hanrahan).
  - Pulmonology not currently planned; will request if any residual
    pulmonary symptoms post-discharge.

## Outstanding items

- Confirm transportation home (husband to drive).
- Medication reconciliation with pharmacy at discharge — particular

  held with reassessment by PCP.
- Patient-and-family education on warning signs (worsening dyspnea,
  fevers, confusion, decreased urine output).

## Plan
Will follow daily. Expect transfer to floor tomorrow morning if
overnight stable, then anticipated 2–3 days on floor before discharge
home with home health.

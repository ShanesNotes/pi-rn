# Physical and occupational therapy — initial evaluation

## Reason for consult
Bedside evaluation post-extubation; reconditioning and discharge
planning.

## Patient status
68-year-old woman, day 3 of ICU admission for severe CAP with septic
shock, status post 47-hour intubation, extubated this morning, now on
HFNC 30 L / 35%. Alert, oriented, conversational, with anticipated
post-ICU deconditioning.

## Baseline functional status (per chart and patient/husband)
- Independent in basic ADLs at home.
- Ambulates with single-point cane outdoors (left ankle sprain 2024,
  did not fully recover) and without assistive device indoors.
- Drives locally without difficulty.
- Walks 15–20 minutes daily and attends water aerobics weekly.
- Lives in a single-story home with one entry step; husband healthy
  and able to assist.
- No prior PT or OT.

## Subjective
She is glad to be off the ventilator. She is mildly fatigued but
motivated. No new pain. No new weakness she can identify. She is
worried about how long she will be in the hospital and wants to "get
moving."

## Objective
- Cardiopulmonary: HR 78, BP 124/68, SpO2 97% on HFNC 30 L / 35%, RR
  16. No work of breathing at rest.
- Strength: 5/5 throughout upper extremities; 4+/5 hip flexors,
  knee extensors bilaterally — modest ICU deconditioning expected
  after 48 hours of bedrest. No focal weakness.
- Range of motion: full throughout, no contractures.
- Sensation: intact to light touch.
- Balance: sitting balance good unsupported; standing balance with
  contact guard at edge of bed.
- Transfers: required minimal assist of 1 to come to edge of bed and
  to stand at side of bed for 30 seconds.
- Ambulation: not attempted today given lines, HFNC, and just-extubated
  status; planned for tomorrow.
- Cognition: oriented x 3, follows multi-step commands.

## Assessment
Predictable ICU deconditioning. No signs of ICU-acquired weakness
beyond expected. No prior functional limits beyond her chronic cane
use. Anticipate full return to baseline with progressive mobilization.

## Plan
- **Today (HD3)**: dangle at edge of bed for 5 minutes once today
  with bedside RN; HOB up for meals.
- **Tomorrow (HD4)**: transfer to chair with contact guard; bedside
  ambulation with rolling walker for safety once HFNC weaned and lines
  permit.
- **HD5+**: progress to corridor ambulation 50 ft, then increasing
  distance daily; advance to single-point cane (her home device) prior
  to discharge.
- Twice-daily PT in ICU and on the floor.
- Will reassess at each visit. Anticipate discharge to home with
  short-course outpatient or home PT.

## Equipment recommendations
- Use rolling walker in hospital for safety.
- At discharge, single-point cane is appropriate for home as it has
  been at her baseline.
- Home environment review with case management does not raise
  concern given single-story home and supportive husband.

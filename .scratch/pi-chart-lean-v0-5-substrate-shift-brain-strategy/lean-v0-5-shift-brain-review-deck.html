Design center

The core product contract

The chart is canonical. Current state is a query. Derived summaries are disposable.
Canonical memoryFacts, actions, notes, refs, provenance, lifecycle, review, and attestation.
Derived projectionsReport sheet, packet, trends, evidence chains, tasks, handoff, care clusters, and shift brain.
Bounded assistantMay cite, summarize, compare, suggest, and prompt review. It does not own truth or completion.

Issues 03–065 / 11
Human authority

Agent boundaries made explicit

The assistant may

  • Summarize source-linked chart context.
  • Cite evidence, uncertainty, source age, and provenance.
  • Suggest tasks, clusters, handoff/watch items, and next sources to review.
  • Explain priority in supportive, nonpunitive language.

The assistant may not

  • Write accepted chart truth directly.
  • Promote suggestions into active workflow without a human.
  • Complete, defer, block, or carry forward tasks autonomously.
  • Finalize handoff or resolve source conflicts.

Closeout

What was deliberately not built

No architecture choiceBackend, storage, runtime, graph/index, semantic search, vector, OpenBrain, access-plane, and adapter are deferred.
No hidden simulator truthNo `pi-sim` internals, oracle labels, validation internals, or hidden patient state as chart source.
No ledger expansion`pi-ledger` remains a reusable kernel; any chart mapping is future adapter-only.
No agent authority jumpNo direct accepted-writes, autonomous completion, final handoff, or conflict resolution.
No UI captureNo generated UI, screenshots, tab names, API shapes, or `_derived` output as substrate authority.
No full EHR cloneNo full CPOE, pharmacy, barcode MAR, legal/compliance, raw audit, ADT, billing, or external EHR integration.

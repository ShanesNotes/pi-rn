## Hot/warm/cold guidance

- **Hot:** Current handoff/watch items, safety excerpts, agent-authored material requiring human acceptance, and note-derived facts promoted into current constraints/problems/open loops through explicit canonical evidence.
- **Warm:** Recent provider/RN notes, communication events, evidence chains, context bundles, memory-proof sections, review/attestation history, and recent narrative supporting active decisions.
- **Cold:** H&P, baseline history, prior encounters, discharge summaries, chronic-disease background, social/family/support context, old consults, and narrative archives. Cold material may be eligible for later semantic retrieval only as an access behavior, not as a backend or second-truth decision.

## Mismatch register inputs

- `NARR-MISMATCH-001`: note/communication subtype split plus section, statement, and quote addressability.
- `NARR-MISMATCH-002`: prior-encounter/cold-history summarization authority, retrieval boundaries, and current-state promotion rules.
- `NARR-MISMATCH-003`: handoff evidence shape, provider-notification closure, preceptor attestation, and A7/A8 structured-vs-narrative boundary.
- `NARR-MISMATCH-004`: generated narrative provenance, agent draft handling, and human acceptance/accountability policy.
- `NARR-MISMATCH-005`: brownfield view DTOs/rendered strings and `_derived` outputs must not become canonical storage.

## v0.5 substrate implication

Narrative context should be charted once as canonical notes, communication, facts, evidence refs, review actions, and attestation actions, then projected many ways into narrative view, evidence chain, memory proof, open loops, care plan, and handoff. Cold longitudinal narrative remains source-linked background until explicitly retrieved or promoted; hot current truth must stay deterministic and auditable without semantic/vector dependence.

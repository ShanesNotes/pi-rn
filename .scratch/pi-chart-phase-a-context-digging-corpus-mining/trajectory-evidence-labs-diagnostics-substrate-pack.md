## Scope and authority stance

This artifact synthesizes the v0.5 substrate needed when a clinician or bounded agent asks: what changed, what evidence supports it, which labs/diagnostics matter, and what results still need review? It reuses the required issue 01 row template and reconciles three approved evidence layers only:

- Source intent from `.scratch/pi-chart-phase-a-context-digging-corpus-mining/source-artifact-mining-map.md`.
- Brownfield implementation evidence from `.scratch/pi-chart-phase-a-context-digging-corpus-mining/issues/03-brownfield-implementation-crosswalk.md`.
- Patient-corpus pressure from `.scratch/pi-chart-phase-a-context-digging-corpus-mining/patient-001-005-corpus-atlas.md`.

Authority interpretation:

- Phase A source rows express clinical/function intent, not implementation authority.
- Brownfield trend/evidence/review code proves attempted behavior and tests, not final v0.5 shape.
- Patient corpus proves scenario pressure and caveats only; hidden or future simulator material is forbidden evidence.
- This is docs-only. It does not authorize source, schema, test, patient, `_derived`, ADR, design, package/lockfile, `pi-ledger`, or `pi-sim` edits.

## Hot/warm/cold synthesis

| Access class | Trajectory/evidence/labs/diagnostics content | Must not depend on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot | Current vital facts and short safety windows; current critical lab/diagnostic result facts; unreviewed critical/contested review state; active deterioration alarms only when chart-visible and evidenced; immediate MAP/lactate/SpO2/K/Cr/current-device context. | Semantic/vector retrieval, hidden simulator state, rendered prototype state, or cold narrative search. |
| Warm | Recent trend windows, evidence chains, artifact/report expansion, result-review history, recent notes supporting diagnostic interpretation, recent labs/CXR/CT/culture/reassessments, proof-fact reuse into handoff/open loops. | Direct promotion to canonical truth or replacement of hot current facts. |
| Cold | Longitudinal labs/imaging/history, prior encounters, chronic disease diagnostic context, old consults/discharge narratives, baseline physiology. | Backend/vector/OpenBrain commitment or a second source of truth. |

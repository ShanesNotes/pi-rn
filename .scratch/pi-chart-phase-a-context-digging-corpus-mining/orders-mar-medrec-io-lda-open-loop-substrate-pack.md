## Scope and authority

This artifact mines orders, MAR, medication reconciliation, intake/output, LDA, oxygen/device context, and open-loop fulfillment as a docs-only substrate pack. It reuses the issue 01 mining template and approved evidence from issue 02 source mining, issue 03 brownfield crosswalk, and issue 04 patient corpus atlas.

Authority stance: source artifacts express clinical/function intent; brownfield code/tests prove attempted behavior and prototype risks only; patient corpus rows show scenario pressure and gaps only. This artifact does not authorize implementation, patient migration, schema changes, full CPOE/pharmacy/MAR/flowsheet products, hidden simulator coupling, vector/backend/OpenBrain choices, direct agent accepted-writes, or pi-ledger expansion.

## Substrate synthesis

- Keep the primitive grammar lean: `intent.order`-like obligations, `action` fulfillments/failures/reviews, observations/context segments for vitals/I&O/LDA/device facts, and notes/artifacts as supporting evidence.
- Treat open-loop state as derived from canonical obligations/actions/observations and chart clock, not as independent truth.
- Keep medication/MAR facts clinically useful: intended medication, administration/hold/refusal/omission/titration evidence, response obligation, reason, reviewer/accountability, and restart/discharge criteria.
- Keep I&O/LDA/device context selective: only facts needed to interpret current status, trend windows, handoff, and evidence chains belong in lean v0.5.
- Preserve hot/warm/cold separation: hot facts must be deterministic current-care context; warm facts support nearby review; cold home-med/prior-history facts must not be required for immediate safety reasoning or imply a backend choice.

## Hot / warm / cold closeout

| Tier | Belongs here | Must not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot | Active medication holds/refusals/critical administrations, active/overdue orders, due source-control or monitoring obligations, current O2/device context, unsafe I&O/volume or line/drain facts, current fall/delirium/safety precautions. | Hot facts must not depend on semantic/vector retrieval, hidden simulator truth, generated UI, or patient fixture convenience. |
| Warm | Pharmacy/consult rationale, serial BMP/CXR/lab evidence, nearby trend windows, review/accountability history, narrative handoff support, current admission med-rec details. | Warm context supports but does not override current hot truth or become independent canonical projection output. |
| Cold | Home medication history, prior intolerance, baseline HFpEF/CKD/AF/diabetes context, prior volume overload, long-term family/support routines. | Cold access is a future access requirement only; it is not a vector, OpenBrain, backend, or external EHR commitment. |

## Deferred or rejected product scope

- Deferred: full CPOE/order entry, pharmacy verification, barcode medication administration, drug dictionary/allergy interaction engine, full protocol/CDS engine, complete flowsheet/device inventory, legal-signature/compliance platform, external medication-history retrieval integrations, and access-plane/backend/vector/OpenBrain implementation.
- Rejected for v0.5 substrate authority: treating rendered MAR/order/task displays as canonical truth; treating `_derived` outputs as truth; using hidden `pi-sim` state or expected actions; expanding pi-ledger kernel requirements from chart adapter pressure; authorizing direct agent accepted-writes.

## Scope and authority stance

This artifact mines the review/accountability substrate family for v0.5 planning only. It reconciles:

- source intent from `.scratch/pi-chart-phase-a-context-digging-corpus-mining/source-artifact-mining-map.md` and the source artifacts cited there;
- brownfield implementation evidence from `.scratch/pi-chart-phase-a-context-digging-corpus-mining/issues/03-brownfield-implementation-crosswalk.md`;
- corpus pressure from `.scratch/pi-chart-phase-a-context-digging-corpus-mining/patient-001-005-corpus-atlas.md`.

The output preserves accountability semantics while keeping schema changes, legal-signature/compliance machinery, role registries, raw access audit, and direct agent accepted-writes out of scope. Review actions are treated as canonical chart facts when explicitly authored; review/accountability states are treated as derived projections unless a later accepted ADR changes that.

## Mismatch-register inputs

1. `REV-MISMATCH-001`: Review-state semantics are well-tested in brownfield but direct patient-corpus coverage for suggested/accepted/verified/rejected/co-signed variants is `not-covered`; later corpus or regression fixtures should cover these states before broad readiness credit.
2. `REV-MISMATCH-002`: Source artifacts pressure attestation/preceptor/generated provenance, but the exact primitive and role vocabulary remain open; do not freeze brownfield communication-attestation shape.
3. `REV-MISMATCH-003`: Authorship/source-kind classes are useful and tested, but brownfield vocabulary duplication may drift; v0.5 should centralize source kinds if implementation is later authorized.
4. `REV-MISMATCH-004`: Legal signature, compliance audit, raw access audit, and role-registry scope are accountability-adjacent but deferred; do not smuggle them into lean v0.5 substrate.
5. `REV-MISMATCH-005`: Direct agent accepted-writes remain out of scope; suggested agent output may be represented only with explicit provenance and human review/acceptance state.
6. `REV-MISMATCH-006`: Corpus operator-review caveats are accountability evidence and must be retained rather than treated as readiness pass/fail noise.
7. `REV-MISMATCH-007`: Transform provenance is required for generated/suggested material, but current patient projection outputs are absent; do not treat generated summaries as canonical truth.
8. `REV-MISMATCH-008`: Active-state/lifecycle behavior is substrate-critical, but structural markdown and filesystem loading are adapter details, not v0.5 storage authority.

## v0.5 substrate guidance

- Carry forward **authorship/source kind**, **explicit review actions**, **attestation/co-sign/witness/scribe facts**, **correction/supersession/entered-in-error/contradiction lifecycle links**, and **operator/human review caveats** as dense accountability substrate.
- Treat **review state**, **attestation chain**, **active claim state**, and **review/caveat banners** as derived projections over canonical facts.
- Keep **suggested agent output** separate from accepted chart truth until a human review/acceptance action exists.
- Keep **legal signature**, **compliance audit**, **raw access audit**, **role-registry machinery**, and **direct agent accepted-write policy** deferred.
- Preserve hot context for safety-sensitive agent-authored, current-care, contested, corrected, or unresolved claims; keep broader audit/review history warm unless current care requires it; treat compliance/history planning as cold/deferred.
